About CP Store

Modern shopping, close to home.

CP Store is a neighbourhood supermarket built around the practical needs of everyday life in Thalakkulathur.

Our purpose

Make the daily shop feel easier.

A supermarket should help people find the necessities of daily life without making the trip complicated.

CP Store brings groceries, pantry basics, home-care products, personal-care items and other useful categories together in one modern local destination. The focus is simple: a convenient place for the neighbourhood's everyday shopping.

Located in Thalakkulathur, Calicut, the store is easy to contact whenever you want to ask about availability before visiting.
